How to keep a lantern lit

Every phase, the moon waxes: the sky grows busier, the moths bolder, and the light sweeter. Chain catches without missing to raise your multiplier — a perfect chain is worth more than a careful one.

Rare white moonblooms open a brief Moonrise: your glow widens, time slows, and every light counts double. Cyan wisps leave a shield that eats one moth. And your dash pulls nearby light straight in.
